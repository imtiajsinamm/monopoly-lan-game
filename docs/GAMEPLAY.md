# Monopoly Game Rules & Gameplay

## Quick Start

1. Each player starts with $1,500
2. Players take turns rolling two dice and moving around the board
3. When you land on an unowned property, you can buy it
4. If you land on an opponent's property, you must pay rent
5. The last player with money wins!

## Board Overview

- **40 Spaces**: 28 properties, 4 corners, 4 railroads, 2 utilities
- **Corners**: GO, Just Visiting (Jail), Free Parking, Go to Jail
- **Properties**: Streets (colored), Railroads, Utilities
- **Special Spaces**: Chance, Community Chest, Income Tax, Luxury Tax

## Turn Sequence

1. **Roll Dice**: Roll two dice and move that many spaces
2. **Land**: Move your token to the new space
3. **Action**: 
   - If space is unowned, you can buy it
   - If owned by opponent, pay rent
   - If special space, follow its rules
4. **End Turn**: Next player's turn (unless you rolled doubles)

## Doubles

If you roll doubles (same number on both dice):
- Move your token
- Take another turn immediately
- If you roll doubles three times in a row, go to jail

## Properties & Money

### Base Rent
Rent is 1/10 of the property purchase price when the property has no improvements.

### Houses & Hotels
- Build houses to increase rent
- 4 houses can be upgraded to 1 hotel
- More buildings = higher rent paid by opponents

### Railroads
- Own 1: $25 per visit
- Own 2: $50 per visit
- Own 3: $100 per visit
- Own 4: $200 per visit

### Utilities
- Rent: 4× the dice roll (if you own 1)
- Rent: 10× the dice roll (if you own both)

## Special Spaces

### GO (Space 0)
- Collect $200 salary when you pass or land here
- This is your starting position

### Free Parking (Space 20)
- Nothing happens (no penalty or reward)

### Go to Jail (Space 30)
- Move directly to Jail
- Do not pass GO, do not collect $200

### Jail (Space 10 - Just Visiting)
You go to jail if you:
- Land on "Go to Jail" space
- Roll doubles three times in one turn

In jail, you can:
- Pay $50 to get out
- Use a "Get Out of Jail Free" card
- Stay in jail for up to 3 turns

### Chance & Community Chest
- Draw a random card
- Cards have various effects (move, pay, collect money)

### Taxes
- **Income Tax**: Pay $200
- **Luxury Tax**: Pay $75

## Buying Properties

When you land on an unowned property:
1. You can choose to buy it at its listed price
2. The property is added to your portfolio
3. Opponents must pay you rent when they land on it
4. You can mortgage properties for half their value if you need cash

## Building Improvements

To build on properties:
1. You must own all properties of the same color
2. Build houses evenly (can't skip)
3. Max 4 houses per property before upgrading to hotel
4. Houses cost varies by color (from $50 to $200)
5. Hotels cost a house + cash payment

## Mortgage

If you need money, you can mortgage properties:
- Receive half the property's value in cash
- Opponent cannot collect rent on mortgaged properties
- Pay back the mortgage (plus 10%) to unmortgage

## Bankruptcy

If you owe more money than you have:
1. Sell properties and improvements
2. Mortgage remaining properties
3. If still unable to pay, you're bankrupt
4. Your properties go to the creditor or back to the bank
5. You're eliminated from the game

## Winning

You win by being the last player with money. All other players must be bankrupt.

## Strategy Tips

1. **Early Game**: Buy properties and avoid overspending
2. **Mid Game**: Build up property monopolies to increase income
3. **Late Game**: Build houses and hotels to bankrupt opponents
4. **Money Management**: Always keep cash reserves for rent payments
5. **Trading**: Negotiate with other players to complete monopolies
6. **Mortgaging**: Use strategically when in cash flow trouble
